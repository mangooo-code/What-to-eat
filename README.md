# What-to-eat 🍳

**English** | [中文](#中文)

Ever stood in front of an open fridge, stomach growling, staring at a lonely egg, half an onion, and a suspicious jar of something, wondering *"what on earth do I cook with THIS?"* — while a delivery app taunts you from your home screen?

**What-to-eat** is your AI-powered kitchen sidekick for exactly that moment. Snap a photo (or just tell it) what's hiding in your fridge, tell it your tastes, allergies, and how much time you've *actually* got, and it whips up recipes tailored to what you already own — no extra grocery run, no 2-hour recipe when you've got 15 minutes, no "surprise, this has peanuts."

- 📸 **Scan your fridge** — snap or speak your ingredients and let AI recognize what you've got
- 🎯 **Tailored to you** — dietary preferences, taste profiles, and household members all shape the suggestions
- ⏱️ **Built for the time-crunched** — recipes that respect your clock, not the other way around
- 🥕 **Zero-waste friendly** — cook with what you have before it goes bad
- 👨‍🍳 **Step-by-step cooking mode** — guided instructions so you don't have to think, just cook

No more decision fatigue, no more wasted groceries — just answer the eternal question, deliciously.

## 中文

有没有过这种时刻：打开冰箱，肚子咕咕叫，眼前只有一个孤零零的鸡蛋、半颗洋葱和一罐来历不明的酱料，脑子里只剩下一个灵魂拷问——"这到底能做出什么菜？" 与此同时，外卖 App 还在手机屏幕上不停向你招手……

**What-to-eat（吃什么）** 就是为这一刻而生的 AI 厨房搭子。拍张照片（或者直接说出来）告诉它冰箱里都有什么，再告诉它你的口味偏好、忌口和"实际能挤出来"的时间，它就能用你现有的食材，生成量身定制的菜谱——不用临时跑腿买菜，不会给你15分钟却推荐2小时的大菜，更不会突然告诉你"这道菜里有花生"。

- 📸 **扫一扫冰箱** —— 拍照或语音输入，AI 自动识别你拥有的食材
- 🎯 **为你量身定制** —— 饮食偏好、口味画像、家庭成员需求，统统纳入推荐逻辑
- ⏱️ **专为时间紧张的人设计** —— 菜谱迁就你的日程，而不是相反
- 🥕 **减少浪费** —— 优先用掉快到期的食材，减少囤积浪费
- 👨‍🍳 **分步烹饪模式** —— 跟着引导一步步做，不用动脑，只管开火

告别选择困难，告别食材浪费——美味地回答那个永恒的问题："今天吃什么？"
